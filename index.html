About Intelliform

This platform uses Artificial Intelligence technologies - speech-to-text, text-to-speech, sign language-to-text, and text-to-sign language - enabling users with disabilities to fill up forms independently.

Organizations can customize their own forms. Users explicitly select how each field is presented and how they provide answers. Completed forms are saved and viewable at any time. Go ahead and explore the form generating platform fitted for you!

Accessibility Statement

Published by team TechInt as Study Project in term 2, semester 6 of the Bachelor of Science in Computer Science course by BITS Pilani.
Last reviewed: 18 July 2026

Our commitment

We are committed to ensuring this platform is accessible to everyone, including people with visual, auditory, motor, cognitive, and speech-related disabilities. Accessibility is built into the product itself - the platform exists to help organisations create accessible forms for their users.

We actively work to meet the requirements of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

Conformance status

Conformant — WCAG 2.2 Level AA

Target standard: Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.
Evaluation date: 18 July 2026.
Evaluation method: Manual audit combined with assistive technology testing (keyboard-only navigation, VoiceOver on macOS, NVDA on Windows).

Technical specification

  • HTML5, CSS, JavaScript - standard web technologies
  • Relies on browser accessibility APIs (ARIA 1.2)
  • Tested in Chrome 126, Firefox 127, Safari 17 on macOS 14 and Windows 11
  • Tested with VoiceOver (macOS/iOS), NVDA 2024, TalkBack (Android)

Measures taken to support accessibility

  • Skip-to-main-content link at the top of every page for keyboard users
  • All interactive elements reachable and operable by keyboard alone
  • Text-resize controls (A− / A+) in the top bar, up to 200% without loss of content
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements (3 px solid, ≥ 3:1 contrast)
  • Full prefers-reduced-motion support - all animations disabled when requested
  • Semantic HTML5 landmarks (<main>, <nav>, <header>, <section>) throughout
  • ARIA live regions announcing dynamic changes (field progress, form status, speech recognition output)
  • Multiple input modalities: typed text, voice (speech recognition), and hand-sign recognition
  • Persona examples illustrating diverse accessibility needs built into the UI
  • Modal dialogs implement focus trapping, aria-modal, and Escape-to-close

Report an accessibility barrier

If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this platform, or if you find something that does not work with your assistive technology, please let us know. We aim to respond within 5 working days and to resolve confirmed issues within 30 days.
